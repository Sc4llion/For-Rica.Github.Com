Dear Rica, Riri, Little Major,

First things first, thank you. Salamat talaga. For the kindness you gave, for the way you care without being asked, and for being the kind of person who makes everyone around her feel safe and seen. Not everyone is like that, and I hope you know how special that makes you. I do not want to take it for granted.

I was a last minute add to the Boscolympics lineup for DBA. No plan, no expectations either. You were there to watch over the students play Football and I remember thinking, blessing talaga toh kese nakasali pa ako. Maybe it’s God’s will. Because on that day, I met you.

And the more I got to know you, the more I realized it was not just the moment or just a feeling. It was you. Your warmth, your love for the people around you, the way you are cute, gorgeous, and kind all at once without even trying. Yung totoo mong pagkatao, yon ang nag-pafall sa akin sayo, Rica.

I call you Little Major, Light Furry, Riri. I know it’s silly but for me, those names just feel right for you. Small, warm, and mine to keep quietly.

I am grateful for you, Rica, I love you dearly. If you ever give me a chance, I will make sure you never regret it.

With love and gratitude,
Miggy
